| D-083 | Email is durable. `send_email_on_commit` keeps its signature (plus optional `kind`, `sensitive`, `related`) but now writes one `OutboundEmail` row per distinct valid recipient inside the caller's transaction and queues the send after commit. A claim (`queued` to `sending` in one UPDATE) makes redelivery harmless; temporary failures retry at 1, 5, 15, 60 and 240 minutes (5 attempts); permanent ones (SMTP 5xx, refused recipient, bad header) fail at once; a dead broker leaves the row queued and the 10-minute sweep sends it. | D-085 | AI calls go through one wrapper (`ai/llm.py`): `generate_json(template_key, variables)`, `embed(texts)`, `transcribe(file)`. | D-088 | System alerts: at most one active alert per kind. `check_alerts` opens, refreshes and resolves them: AI failures above 20% with at least 10 calls in 15 minutes (critical), emails due for more than 30 minutes (warning, critical from 20), and no worker heartbeat.
