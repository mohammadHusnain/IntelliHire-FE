# Jobs To Be Done

These jobs describe what each person needs to get done whether or not this app exists. None of them mentions a screen, button or feature.

**J-1 (R-1 Everyday Calculator User).**
"When I need a figure for a purchase, a receipt or my budget, I want to work it out quickly and be sure it is right, so I can act on it without doing the sum again by hand."

**J-2 (R-2 Student).**
"When I am solving a multi-step maths or science problem, I want to evaluate expressions with powers, roots, logarithms and angles exactly as they are written, so I can check my working and find where I went wrong."

**J-3 (R-3 Prospective Borrower).**
"When I am deciding whether to borrow a large sum, I want to know what it will cost me each month and in total, so I can judge whether I can afford it and compare offers."

**J-4 (R-4 Group Bill Payer).**
"When a group meal ends with one bill, I want to settle a fair tip and what each person owes, so I can collect the right amount without anyone overpaying or arguing."
